Off the Clock

The person behind the credentials.

I came to nursing the long way. First degree in biology and sociology, NCAA cross country and track along the way — then the realization that what I actually wanted was work where the stakes are human. Nursing school in Michigan, night shifts in trauma and neuro ICUs, and eventually a one-way move from the Midwest to Palo Alto.

The through-line has always been teaching and fixing systems: precepting new ICU nurses, chairing unit committees, building shadowing programs — and eventually realizing the biggest fixable system was the software itself. The distance-runner mindset never left: I'm at my best on long, patient problems that reward showing up every day.

This site is one of those problems. I taught myself to code the same way I built StaffQuery — by shipping. Scroll down to the colophon to see what's under the hood.
